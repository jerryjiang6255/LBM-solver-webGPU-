Educational use only. This simulator demonstrates a D2Q9 Lattice Boltzmann Method flow solver with selectable collision and LES models, pulsing inlet forcing, BFL wall handling, compare mode, monitor plotting, and sandbox wall drawing. The WebGPU version preserves the same intended external behavior while moving the core solver and field rendering onto the GPU for higher performance.
